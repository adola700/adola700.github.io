A more natural voice for Hinglish

Fine-tuning Veena for mixed Hindi–English speech, with an open training and streaming pipeline.

Akhila Datta DolaPublished 26 September 2026

Hinglish brings Hindi and English together in everyday conversation. Speech models need to handle that switching naturally.

Fine-tuning for the way people speak

This project fine-tunes Veena with LoRA on Hinglish data and uses SNAC audio tokenization. In the evaluation reported in the release, mean opinion score improved from 4.12 to 4.66 on Hinglish. These are project evaluation results, rather than a universal measure of voice quality.

The pipeline is open

The release includes training, evaluation, and real-time streaming inference code. The aim is to make the work reproducible and give others a starting point for further speech experiments.
